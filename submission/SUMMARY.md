# Solution Summary

WildGuard AI is a state-of-the-art wildlife conflict mitigation platform submitted for the eSewa x WWF Hackathon 2026. The solution leverages Gemini 1.5 Flash Vision to transform static camera trap imagery into a dynamic Early Warning System (EWS). By identifying high-risk species (Tigers, Elephants, Rhinos) with sub-second latency, the system autonomously triggers hyper-local SMS alerts and acoustic deterrents, reducing the response time to conflict by over 80%.

Distinctively, WildGuard AI bridges the gap between conservation and fintech. Through a deep integration with eSewa, it facilitates an "Instant Relief" mechanism where AI-verified crop or livestock damage claims result in immediate financial payouts to affected farmers' digital wallets. This ensures community resilience and fosters a positive relationship with wildlife conservation. The platform features a high-impact "Mission Control" dashboard, interactive geospatial risk mapping, and a real-time incident terminal. Scalable across global national parks, WildGuard AI represents a paradigm shift in protecting endangered species while securing human livelihoods in the buffer zones of the world's most critical ecosystems.
