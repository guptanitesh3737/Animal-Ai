# WildGuard AI - Demo Video Script (3-5 Minutes)

## 0:00 - 0:45 | Intro & Problem
"Every year, hundreds of families in buffer zones living near our world-class national parks wake up to devastated fields or worse. Human-wildlife conflict isn't just a conservation issue; it's a poverty issue. Today, we introduce WildGuard AI."

## 0:45 - 2:00 | The Solution (Detection)
"Here is the WildGuard Command Center. On the monitor, we see real-time stats from across the sector. Let’s simulate a detection. I’ll upload this thermal image from a remote camera trap. 
*Action: Upload tiger image in Scanner View*
As you see, the Gemini 1.5 neural engine identifies the Bengal Tiger instantly, calculates a 98% confidence score, and tags this as 'CRITICAL RISK'."

## 2:00 - 3:00 | The Alert (EWS)
"Because it's a critical threat, our system doesn't just wait. It automatically triggers our Early Warning System. A warning blast is sent to 4,000 devices in the immediate vicinity via SMS, and localized sirens are activated to safely deter the animal back to the deep forest."

## 3:00 - 4:00 | Fintech & Impact
"And for when losses do occur, we've integrated eSewa. Within seconds of verification, a farmer can receive relief funds directly to their eSewa wallet, ensuring no one is forced to choose between their survival and the tiger's survival. 
*Action: Show Finance View history*
This is the future of conservation: data-driven, community-focused, and financially sustainable. This is WildGuard AI."

## 4:00 - 4:15 | Closing
"Join us in protecting our wildlife and our people, one alert at a time. Thank you."
