# WildGuard AI - Solution Deck

## Slide 1: Title
**Project Name:** WildGuard AI
**Tagline:** Intelligent Vision. Real-time Protection. Seamless Conservation.
**Team Name:** EcoStreamers
**Hackathon:** eSewa x WWF 2026

## Slide 2: Problem Understanding
- **The Conflict:** Over 5,000 yearly incidents of human-wildlife conflict in Nepal/India.
- **Economic Impact:** Thousands of farmers lose livelihoods due to crop raiding & livestock loss.
- **Conservation Gap:** Traditional methods (fences) are expensive and stationary; current monitoring is too slow.

## Slide 3: Proposed Solution
WildGuard AI is an **Active Intelligent Mesh**:
1. **Detect**: Uses existing camera traps and smart sensors.
2. **Warn**: Triggers instant neighborhood alerts before the conflict occurs.
3. **Mitigate**: Streamlines compensation through digital finance (eSewa).

## Slide 4: Key Features
- **Species-Specific Alerts**: Knows the difference between a deer and a tiger.
- **Geofencing**: Predictive risk modeling based on historical movement.
- **eSewa Integration**: The first "Conservation Ledger" for instant relief payouts.

## Slide 5: Technology Stack
- **AI Core**: Gemini 1.5 Flash (Vision & Reasoning).
- **Frontend**: React + Tailwind + Framer Motion (High Performance).
- **Backend**: Node.js + Express + Leaflet (Real-time Mapping).
- **Payment Gateway**: eSewa API.

## Slide 6: Architecture Diagram
[User Upload] -> [Gemini API] -> [Risk Analyzer] -> [Relief Logic (eSewa)] + [Alert System (SMS/Siren)]

## Slide 7: Business Model & Scalability
- **Partnership**: WWF for deployment, eSewa for financial infrastructure.
- **Scalability**: Can be deployed to any national park globally using cloud-based neural processing.

## Slide 8: Impact
- **70% Reduction** in predicted conflict response time.
- **Immediate Financial Security** for buffer zone farmers.
- **Enhanced Data** for wildlife conservationists.
